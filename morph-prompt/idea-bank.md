# Dave's Idea Bank: iPhone tips

Every idea has a number. Say "idea #7" and Claude opens this file and uses it.
Status: NEW = not used yet · KNOWN = viewers already know it (weak) · USED = already made into an episode
Before filming any idea: check the steps on a real iPhone with the latest iOS. Menus change between versions.

## 🟦 WORK SMARTER

### #1 · One tap starts your whole workday (Shortcuts)
- **Hook:** "Stop opening 5 apps every morning."
- **Why:** One tap, a Siri request or an automatic trigger runs a whole routine.
- **How:** Shortcuts → Gallery for starters, or build your own. Automations can run at a time, a place, when an app opens, or when a device connects.
- **Episode angle:** A "Start Work" shortcut: turns on Work Focus → opens Calendar and Reminders → shows your next meeting → opens Slack/Teams → starts your focus playlist.
- **Status:** NEW ⭐ strong episode

### #2 · Work Focus that turns itself on
- **Hook:** "Your phone can stop distracting you, automatically."
- **Why:** Blocks personal alerts during work, but the people who matter can still reach you.
- **How:** Settings → Focus → Work. Pick allowed people and apps, link a clean Home Screen, and schedule it by time, place, or when an app opens.
- **Status:** NEW

### #3 · Text replacement for things you type all the time
- **Hook:** "Type 4 letters, get a whole paragraph."
- **Why:** Email address, meeting link, home address, common replies.
- **How:** Settings → General → Keyboard → Text Replacement. Example: `;meet` becomes "Here's my scheduling link, pick any time that works for you: …"
- **Status:** NEW ⭐ strong "whoa" demo

### #4 · Hide distracting Home Screen pages
- **Hook:** "Make your games disappear during work hours."
- **How:** Long-press an empty spot on the Home Screen → tap the page dots → untick the pages to hide. Pair it with a Focus mode.
- **Status:** NEW

## 🟩 MESSAGES & REMINDERS

### #5 · Schedule a text to send later
- **Hook:** "Never forget a birthday text again."
- **How:** In an iMessage chat, tap + → Send Later → pick the time → type → send. Up to 14 days ahead.
- **Status:** NEW ⭐ strong, most people don't know it

### #6 · "Remind me when I message this person"
- **Hook:** "Your phone reminds you, right when you text them."
- **Example:** "Ask Mom about the recipe" pops up the next time you message Mom.
- **How:** New reminder → details → turn on When Messaging → pick the contact.
- **Status:** NEW ⭐

### #7 · Location reminders
- **Hook:** "A reminder that waits until you're at the store."
- **Examples:** "Buy batteries" when near Target, "Submit timesheet" when leaving work.
- **How:** In Reminders, set the alert to a location (arriving or leaving).
- **Status:** NEW

### #8 · Shared family or team lists
- **Hook:** "One grocery list the whole family can edit."
- **How:** Reminders → share a list. Everyone adds and ticks off items, and you can assign tasks with @Name.
- **Status:** NEW

## 🟨 EVERYDAY GEMS

### #9 · Scan documents straight into Notes
- **Hook:** "You don't need a scanner app."
- **How:** Open a note → attachment/camera button → Scan Documents. It finds the page edges and saves a PDF.
- **Use for:** receipts, signed forms, whiteboards, handwritten notes.
- **Status:** NEW (fairly well known)

### #10 · Copy text from real life (Live Text)
- **Hook:** "Copy the Wi-Fi password straight off the sign."
- **How:** Point the Camera at text → tap the text button → select → copy or share.
- **Use for:** signs, business cards, serial numbers, recipes, Wi-Fi passwords.
- **Status:** NEW ⭐ very visual

### #11 · Search inside your photos and scans
- **Hook:** "Find that receipt by typing one word."
- **How:** Notes search covers typed text, handwriting, objects in images, and text in scanned documents.
- **Status:** NEW (pairs well with #9)

## 🟪 AI ON YOUR IPHONE (Apple Intelligence)
Only on supported iPhones, iOS versions, languages and regions. Always say "if your iPhone supports it."
Turn on: Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

### #12 · Writing Tools: fix any message
- **Hook:** "Turn a messy text into a perfect one."
- **How:** Select text → Writing Tools → proofread, rewrite, change the tone, or summarize.
- **Demo:** a rushed 120-word message → a polite 40-word one.
- **Warning to include:** always read it before sending.
- **Status:** NEW ⭐

### #13 · Notification summaries
- **Hook:** "50 notifications turned into one sentence."
- **How:** Apple Intelligence summarizes and prioritizes alerts. Pair it with a Focus mode.
- **Status:** NEW

### #14 · Image Playground
- **Hook:** "Make a picture from a sentence, on your phone."
- **Use for:** party invites, class material, social posts.
- **Status:** NEW

### #15 · Genmoji: make your own emoji
- **Hook:** "Make an emoji that doesn't exist."
- **How:** Create a custom emoji from a text prompt, or from people in your photos where supported.
- **Status:** NEW ⭐ fun, very visual

### #16 · Visual Intelligence
- **Hook:** "Ask your phone what you're looking at."
- **How:** On supported iPhones, get info about what's on your screen or in front of the camera.
- **Status:** NEW
